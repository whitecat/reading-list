# Privacy Policy

**Reading List doesn't log or share your personal information.**

We have nowhere to store your data. We do not track you. We do not profile you. There are no analytics.

- Your saved pages and settings stay in your browser's extension storage. If you're signed in to your browser, the browser syncs them between your devices through your browser account. They are never sent to us.
- When a saved page has no icon of its own, the extension asks DuckDuckGo for one, sending only the site's hostname (for example, `example.com`).
